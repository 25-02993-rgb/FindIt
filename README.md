Project Overview

Students, faculty, and staff sometimes lose things like ID cards, wallets, keys, phones, and school materials while inside the campus. Right now, the information is spread across social media posts, group chats, and word of mouth, so it is hard to search and often doesn't reach the people who are near the place where the item was last seen.

FindIt gives the campus one place to post lost and found items. Reports show up on a campus map, and the app can tell users when a report is near them so they can follow up and return the item safely.

Features
Campus Access: Made for the students, faculty, and staff of Batangas State University–Alangilan Campus.
Lost and Found Reports: Submit a report with a photo, description, category, date, and approximate location. Lost items and found items have separate reports to keep things organized.
Campus Map: Reported items appear on a map so you can browse by location. A campus landmark selector can be used together with GPS pins, especially for indoor areas.
Nearby Reports: The app checks for reports within about 100 meters of your location. Accuracy depends on GPS, permissions, and internet connection.
Notifications: Get an alert when there is a report near you. Background notifications depend on Android permissions and system restrictions, so they may not always arrive.
Chat: Owners and finders can message each other in the app to check if an item is a match and plan how to return it.
Report Status and Moderation: Users can mark a report as resolved. An admin or moderator can review reports that look suspicious or inappropriate.
